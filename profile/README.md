# 🧪 HexaCloudLab

**HexaCloudLab is the research and development laboratory of HexaCloud.**

This organization hosts experimental projects, internal tools, proofs of concept, and open-source initiatives focused on infrastructure, backend systems, real-time communication, observability, and developer platforms.

---

## 🎯 Purpose

HexaCloudLab exists to:

- Explore new infrastructure architectures and patterns
- Validate ideas before they reach production
- Develop internal and open-source tools
- Document technical decisions and learnings
- Serve as the technical knowledge base of HexaCloud

Not every project here is a finished product. Many repositories are **experiments**, **technical MVPs**, or **foundational building blocks**.

---

## 🧠 What you’ll find here

Repositories in HexaCloudLab may include:

- Backend services and APIs
- Control planes and dashboards
- Real-time systems (WebSocket, events, messaging)
- Authentication and access control systems
- Observability, metrics, and logging tools
- Deployment and automation utilities
- Performance and scalability experiments
- Public landing pages and UI prototypes
- Technical documentation and architectural notes

---

## 🧱 Relationship to HexaCloud

- **HexaCloudLab** → Research, experimentation, prototyping, and open source  
- **HexaCloud** → Product, platform, and commercial services

Successful projects in the Lab may:
- Be integrated into HexaCloud
- Evolve into standalone products
- Remain open-source tools or references
